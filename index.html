Wird: Daily Quran

A gentle way to keep a daily portion of the Quran: one page a day in the Madinah mushaf, with its words.

Get help

Questions, a word or page that looks wrong, or an idea: email wird.app.support@gmail.com. Testers can also send feedback from inside TestFlight by taking a screenshot in the app.

Privacy policy · Wird collects no data. Your reading stays on your iPhone.

Common questions

I missed a day. Is my streak gone?
Not necessarily. Freezes cover missed days automatically, and after a missed day the app offers (never requires) a few extra pages to catch up.
Can I listen without internet?
Yes. Your next week of reading is saved automatically on Wi-Fi, and you can download any juz from Mushaf → Offline audio.
How do I change the language, reciter or reminder time?
Tap ⋯ on the Today screen. Language, appearance, reciter, pages per day and reminders are all there.
Where does the text come from?
The Quran text is from Tanzil, checked word by word; the page layout follows the 604-page Madinah mushaf; tafsir, word meanings (English, and Arabic from al-Muyassar fi Gharib al-Quran) and roots come from QUL by Tarteel; recitation audio from Islamic Network.
